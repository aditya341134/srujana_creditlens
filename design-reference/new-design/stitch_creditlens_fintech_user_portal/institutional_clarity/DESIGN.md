## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rigor, equity, and transparency required of next-generation institutional consumer finance. Its primary objective is to make complex algorithmic underwriting feel fair, mathematically sound, and actionable for borrowers and lending officers alike.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Institutional Banking** with **Precision Data Density**:
- **Demeanor:** Lucid, measured, and impartial. Avoid emotional visual ploys, high-saturation gradients, playful gamification, or crypto-native tropes.
- **Visual Stance:** Ultra-crisp architectural structure, balanced micro-metrics, unambiguous typography, and high visual hierarchy.
- **Trust Metaphor:** Clean paper-like slate and pure white surfaces, razor-sharp hairline borders, and structural navy structural anchors that evoke long-standing solvency and statutory compliance.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to present statutory clarity, objective financial telemetry, and distinct status differentiation without sensory fatigue.

### Color Roles & Semantics
- **Primary Anchor (`#1e3a8a` / `#0f2b5c`):** Deep Sovereign Blue. Applied to master navigation bars, institutional signifiers, key interactive calls-to-action, active segment pills, and principal data focus points.
- **Secondary / Positive Telemetry (`#0f766e` / `#10b981`):** Deep Emerald & Crisp Teal. Reserved strictly for positive financial attributes, verified debt obligations, clean credit repayment trends, score upward trajectory, and debt-to-income surplus indicators.
- **Tertiary / Attention & Advisory (`#d97706` / `#f59e0b`):** Balanced Amber. Communicates unverified external accounts, pending credit bureau dispute flags, marginal threshold limits, and conditional approval notices.
- **Negative & Risk Vector (`#e11d48` / `#dc2626`):** Controlled Crimson. Used deliberately for hard limits, delinquent lines, charge-offs, risk alerts, and form validation rejections.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, `#f1f5f9`, `#e2e8f0`, `#0f172a`):** Crisp pure whites for foreground cards, cool slate-50 (`#f8fafc`) canvas backgrounds, delicate structural dividers (`#e2e8f0`), and deep slate-900 (`#0f172a`) for high-legibility display typography.

## Typography

The type hierarchy relies on `Inter` across all structural tiers, prioritizing exceptional tabular clarity, neutral tone, and effortless scanning across large financial tables and explainability audits.

- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** Enforce OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across all numerical metrics, debt calculators, amortization rows, and balance charts to ensure vertical decimal alignment.
- **Uppercase Labels:** The `label-sm` role uses standard uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.04em`) exclusively for status tags, section pre-headers, bureau flags, and table column headers.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Strict optical contrast between bold labels (`600`) and tabular data (`400`/`700`) prevents reading friction during multi-attribute credit file assessments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an institutional 12-column responsive grid with a strict 4px/8px incremental base. 

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Max layout container capped at 1440px with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Primary layout employs a persistent fixed left-side institutional navigational dock (260px) paired with a responsive 12-column workspace split into main evaluation telemetry (8 cols) and explainability factor audit panels (4 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Navigation folds to a compact collapsible rail. Secondary audit panels stack below the primary telemetry summary.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout. Gutters compress to `1rem`, outer margins to `1rem`. Factor analysis stacks sequentially into modular swipeable cards or explicit vertical accordions.
- **Rhythm:** Dense yet breathable. Critical telemetry metrics use `space-md` inner padding, while section boundaries maintain `space-xl` separation to avoid visual collision.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects heavy drop shadows, colored glow rings, and glassy transparency. Depth is expressed structurally through flat tonal surfaces and hairline precision borders:

- **Base Floor (Canvas):** Tone `#f8fafc`. Unbroken neutral foundation for the entire viewport.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Pure `#ffffff` background with a crisp 1px hairline border in `#e2e8f0`. Zero blur shadow under default state (`box-shadow: none`).
- **Layer 2 (Overlays, Flyouts, & Active Modals):** Pure `#ffffff` surface, bounded by 1px `#cbd5e1` outline with a clinical, whisper-subtle ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** 1px hairline rules in `#f1f5f9` (internal card separations) and `#e2e8f0` (canvas component boundaries).

## Shapes

Shapes reflect precision-milled architectural standards:

- **Corner Radii:** Standard component geometry utilizes a restrained 8px radius (`0.5rem`). Outer metric cards and major dashboard panels step up to 12px (`0.75rem`), while micro tags, badges, and progress bar tracks scale down to 4px–6px.
- **Interactive State Predictability:** Active inputs, buttons, and dropdowns retain an identical base corner radius across all states, preventing layout shift or optical warping during interaction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1e3a8a` background, white text (`Inter` 600), 8px border radius, 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05). Hover: `#172554`. Focus: 2px offset with 2px ring in `#3b82f6`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** `#ffffff` background, 1px border `#cbd5e1`, `#1e293b` text. Hover: `#f8fafc` background, `#94a3b8` border.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Transparent background, `#475569` text. Hover: `#f1f5f9` fill, `#0f172a` text.

### Explainability Badges & Status Chips
- **Neutral / Context:** `#f1f5f9` background, `#475569` text, 1px `#e2e8f0` border.
- **Positive / Verified:** `#f0fdf4` background, `#166534` text, 1px border in `#bbf7d0`. Left-aligned with an emerald micro status dot.
- **Advisory / Moderate Factor:** `#fffbeb` background, `#b45309` text, 1px border in `#fde68a`.
- **Adverse / Risk Factor:** `#fef2f2` background, `#991b1b` text, 1px border in `#fecaca`.

### Metric Data Cards
- Container built from pure `#ffffff` with 1px `#e2e8f0` stroke and 12px roundedness.
- Top section contains `label-sm` title in `#64748b` accompanied by an optional tooltip trigger.
- Center displays `metric-xl` figure in `#0f172a` with tabular digit alignment.
- Bottom features comparative metadata (e.g., "vs Bureau Average") paired with a directional delta badge.

### Factor Attribution Progress Bars
- 6px height track with `#e2e8f0` fill and 3px rounded caps.
- Dynamic fill indicator maps to factor impact: positive impact (`#0d9488`), baseline/neutral (`#1e3a8a`), warning (`#d97706`), or critical deficit (`#e11d48`).
- Segmented bars include 2px white dividing gaps to indicate multi-tiered limits.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** 40px height, `#ffffff` surface, 1px `#cbd5e1` border, 8px border radius. Placeholder in `#94a3b8`. Focus: `#1e3a8a` border with a 3px soft tint ring (`rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px box, `#ffffff` surface, 1px `#cbd5e1` border. Checked: `#1e3a8a` fill with crisp white vector check/dot.

### Factor Impact Tree (Explainability Module)
- A specialized tabular list component detailing the exact positive and negative drivers of a credit decision (e.g., "Revolving Utilization < 9%", "Zero Late Payments (24mo)").
- Left edge features a 3px vertical accent border indicating factor directionality (Emerald for credit accretive, Crimson for credit dilutive).
